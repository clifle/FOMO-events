Scope: replacement visual system for the FOMO Events home, Torino calendar, Genova calendar, and event submission page. Read-mode web surfaces for residents and visitors exploring specific upcoming cultural, art, music, food, and community events.

Audience: people deciding what to do in Torino/Piemonte or Genova/Liguria. Job: scan dated listings, filter and search, open verified event sources, locate venues on a map, or submit an event for review. Preserve the existing event content, date filters, category filters, map, city switcher, source links, donation link, and email form.

## Direction contract

THESIS: FOMO Events is a useful illustrated field guide to what is happening in two cities. Its recognizable skyline illustrations lead into exact, date-first event listings; the standard generic events grid is not the identity.

OWN-WORLD: Clean, contemporary vector illustration with decisive silhouettes and restrained geometric detail. Torino is anchored in #305CDE and Genova in #024345; each gets a distinct landmark-and-water/city composition. Use those accents on a light neutral field, strong readable text, and consistent editorial type and spacing. No photography.

STORY: Visitors identify their city immediately, understand what is on today and soon, then browse or filter the dated listings, follow event-specific sources, use the map, or submit an event. The city switcher and submission route remain easy to find.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A shared FOMO masthead and city navigation sit above a city-specific title and compact orientation copy. A wide, original city illustration is the main visual moment, with the city color carrying its large shapes. Date/search/category controls and the first dated events follow directly beneath it; the illustration never displaces the calendar.

FORM: Illustrated Field Guide, assigned direction 6; seed:57a40a0ca89a76ef. Use a purpose-drawn Torino skyline with Mole, Alpine horizon and Po cues; use a separate Genova harbor composition with Lanterna, old-port arcades and hillside buildings. Reuse the illustration grammar in the home and submission surfaces without making them look like event pages.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
