# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Residents and visitors in Torino and Genova who want to find things to do in town.

## Product Purpose

FOMO Events helps people find specific, dated culture, art, music, food and community events in Torino and Genova, with source links, a map and event submissions. It is curated and not exhaustive.

## Positioning

City calendars bring together date-specific listings from public city, regional, venue, festival, museum, editorial, organizer and social sources, with links to the event information and map locations.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse by city, date and category, search event titles and venues, open organizer details, and use the map to explore nearby listings. Organizers can submit events through the shared event form and choose a city.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static web pages for Torino and Genova, with date and category filters, search, event maps and a shared event-submission page.
- Calendar entries should identify a specific event and date, link to a credible event detail or organizer source, and show the full date range for multi-day events.
- Coverage is curated, not exhaustive. Public social discovery is partial; content behind login is not collected.
- Users should verify dates, tickets and access with event organizers.

## Brand Commitments

- The site name is FOMO Events.
- Torino uses electric blue `#305CDE`; Genova uses green `#024345`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Current source pages: `torino/index.html`, `genova/index.html`, `submit.html`, `thanks.html`, and the root landing page.
- Existing event listings, organizer/source links, map UI and event submission flow are project evidence. Do not invent endorsements, usage statistics or event details.

## Product Principles

- Prefer specific, dated event listings over general venue or festival frameworks.
- Keep event details traceable to a credible source.
- Make city and date selection straightforward.
- Be clear that the guide is curated and public-source coverage is incomplete.
